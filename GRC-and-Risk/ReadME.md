A collection of practical Governance, Risk and Compliance projects demonstrating my ability to identify and assess cybersecurity risks, evaluate security controls, analyse compliance gaps, and develop risk-based remediation recommendations.

**Northgate Accountancy** Ltd is a fictional UK accountancy practice created for cybersecurity GRC portfolio projects.

The case study provides a realistic business environment for applying cybersecurity risk management, governance, security frameworks, regulatory requirements, and control assessments.

## Projects

### 1. Cybersecurity Risk Assessment
Identification and assessment of cybersecurity risks, existing controls, risk ratings, treatment options, and remediation priorities.

**Skills:** Risk Assessment | Risk Register | Risk Scoring | Control Analysis | Risk Treatment | Executive Reporting

### 2. NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0 Gap Assessment
Assessment of the organisation's cybersecurity maturity against the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, including gap identification and remediation planning.

**Skills:** NIST CSF 2.0 | Gap Analysis | Security Maturity | Control Assessment | Remediation Planning
