# Northgate Accountancy Ltd — GRC Case Study

Northgate Accountancy Ltd is a fictional UK accountancy practice created for practical Governance, Risk and Compliance (GRC) portfolio projects.

This case study demonstrates the practical application of cybersecurity risk management, security frameworks, control assessments, compliance requirements, and remediation planning.

## Projects

### 1. Cybersecurity Risk Assessment
A comprehensive assessment of cybersecurity risks, existing controls, risk ratings, treatment options, and remediation priorities.

### 2. NIST CSF 2.0 Gap Assessment
An assessment of the organisation's cybersecurity posture against the NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0, identifying security gaps and developing prioritised remediation recommendations.
